Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

Introduction

Welcome to LLash. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use our application.

Information We Collect

LLash requires an account. We collect only the information needed to provide the service:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password when you sign up. Passwords are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Audio files: music files you choose to upload for processing (such as splitting audio) and the resulting files generated from them.
  • Purchase information: when you buy credits in the app, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase token and order identifier to confirm the purchase and update your balance. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Wallet activity: your credit balance and transaction history.

LLash does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone, and it does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To process, store, and return the audio files you submit.
  • To manage your credits and verify purchases.
  • To send account-related messages, such as password recovery.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

LLash stores your session token and Dropbox access tokens in secure, encrypted storage on your device. Your library and app settings are kept in a local database on your device.

Third-Party Services

  • Dropbox (optional): if you connect your Dropbox account, LLash uploads and downloads backup files in your Dropbox on your behalf. Dropbox's handling of your data is governed by the Dropbox Privacy Policy. You can disconnect Dropbox at any time.
  • Google Play Billing: used to process in-app purchases, governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and files for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, and we will remove it within a reasonable period, except where we must keep information to comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and sensitive tokens are stored in encrypted storage on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

LLash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Acceptance of Terms

By using LLash, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms of use.
